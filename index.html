Features

Look it up. Make it stick.

Search any Japanese word, save it as a flashcard, and review until you own it.

Search any word

Type kanji, kana, or rōmaji and get a clean result the moment you start typing — reading, meaning, and an example sentence, ready to read at a glance.

Turn it into a flashcard

Save any word to your flashcards in a single tap. Review with spaced repetition and watch a sakura tree bloom as your deck grows.

Synced across devices

Your words and progress stay in sync between Mac and iPhone — pick up exactly where you left off.

AI study tools

Generate study sessions from your own words, check whether a sentence sounds natural, and get clear explanations on demand.

JLPT practice

Timed exam simulations with scored results, from N5 to N1. Know where you stand before test day.

Business Japanese

Keigo, email phrasing, meetings, and interview practice — a full course path for the office.

Mitsuki Pro

When you're ready to go further.

The core of Mitsuki — search, flashcards, and sync — is always free. Pro unlocks the heavier AI features for when you want them.

  • Full AI-generated study sessions, built around your own words
  • AI custom decks from any topic or document

Privacy

Your study life is nobody's business.

Works without an account

Search and study work right on your device. Sign in only when you want your flashcards to sync across devices.

Your data, your account

Your flashcards and progress are protected by per-user access rules — only your account can read your data.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising or analytics SDKs. Mitsuki does not track you across apps or websites — there is nothing to opt out of.
