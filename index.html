What's the difference with いつも?
絶えず is literary — "constantly, without pause", like a river. いつも is the everyday "always" — habits, not flow.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you might want to know before you start.

Yes. Searching the dictionary and building flashcards with spaced repetition are free, with no ads and no account required. Mitsuki Pro adds optional AI study tools and higher daily limits.

Mitsuki is a native Mac app today, with iPhone coming soon. When you sign in, your words and review progress sync across your devices, so you can add on Mac and review on iPhone.

No. Mitsuki has its own built-in flashcards and review engine. If you already use Anki, the Mitsuki Extension can capture subtitle lines while you watch — but Anki is entirely optional.

Cards come back on a spaced-repetition schedule that adapts to how well you remember each word. Rate a card Again, Hard, Good, or Easy and Mitsuki spaces the next review to match — more often for the words you find hard, less for the ones you know.

Your study stays on your device until you choose to sync. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no cross-app tracking. When you do sign in, your flashcards and progress are tied to your account only. See the full Privacy Policy for details.

Lookups are powered by JMdict/EDICT, the open Japanese–English dictionary maintained by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group. You can search in kanji, kana, or rōmaji and get the reading, meaning, and an example at a glance.
